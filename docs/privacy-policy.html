Privacy Policy – Glossia

Last updated: March 18, 2026

Glossia ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

  • Account information: email address and username, used for authentication and to display your profile.
  • Profile photo: optional, uploaded by you and displayed in your profile and friends list.
  • Gameplay data: your scores, streak, and game history, stored to power leaderboards and your profile.
  • Audio recordings: your 30-second speech is sent to OpenAI's Whisper API for transcription and analysis. It is not stored on our servers after processing.

How We Use Your Data

All data collected is used solely to provide app functionality: authentication, displaying your profile and stats, and enabling social features (friends, podium). We do not use your data for advertising or marketing.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, database, and file storage.
  • OpenAI: speech transcription and analysis.

Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account exists. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Glossia is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
